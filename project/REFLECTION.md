# 🤖 AI Reflection – Semester Review

## 🧠 About Your Process

### Biggest "Aha Moment"
Learning that being more specific in prompts gets you much closer to the desired outcome.

### When AI Gave Something Useless
When I was too general, the AI would either give something overly complicated or too minimal, depending on the tool.  
I adjusted my prompt to be more specific or switched to a different AI.

### How My Prompting Changed
From Week 1 to now, I learned that no matter which AI you use, being specific usually gives better results.  
I also learned that AI can make mistakes, so double-checking outputs is important—whether it's code or everyday questions.

---

## 📚 About What You Learned

### New Skill Gained
One thing I can do now that I couldn’t do in August is structure prompts in a way that gets not just answers, but explanations as well.

### Biggest Surprise
What surprised me most is how different AI tools are.  
Using the exact same prompt across different tools can produce very different results.

### Where AI Falls Short
One thing AI is genuinely bad at (in my experience): recipes.  
I tried using it for meal inspiration, and the results weren’t very helpful.

## 🔮 About What’s Next

### Future Use of AI
I plan to use AI as a support tool in other classes and work, especially for remembering commands I don’t use often—like Linux or Codespaces.

### Advice for Beginners
It depends on what they’re trying to do.  
Based on that, I’d recommend choosing the AI tool that fits their specific need.

### What I Want to Learn More About
As AI use grows, I’d like to learn how to tell if something is legitimate or AI-generated, especially in cases like scams or misinformation.
